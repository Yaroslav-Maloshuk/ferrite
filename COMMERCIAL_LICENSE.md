2. **OEM / embedded use** — bundling Ferrite inside your own product, appliance,

3. **Support SLA** — defined response-time commitments, hotfix priorities, and

4. **Warranty/indemnification** — a contract you can show a compliance team,

## Relationship to the open-source core

None of Ferrite's features are stripped from the MIT release. The Commercial
License buys **legal coverage and support**, not a feature gate — your buyer's
procurement team gets the contract they need, your engineers still get MIT source
they can inspect and fork.
